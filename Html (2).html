    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I book a transfer?

    Fill in the booking form on this page or message us directly on WhatsApp. We will reply with your price and confirmation.

    Are the prices fixed?

    The prices shown are starting rates. Your final price depends on your pick-up and drop-off location and is confirmed before the trip — no hidden fees.

    What if my flight is delayed?

    Please send us your flight number when booking so our driver can adjust the pick-up time.

    How do I pay?

    You can pay in cash (Thai Baht) to the driver or by bank transfer. Please ask us for other payment options.

    Can I request a child seat?

    Yes, please let us know when booking. Subject to availability.
